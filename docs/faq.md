# FinAssist FAQ
## What can FinAssist do?
FinAssist can answer questions about spending, transaction history, categories, budgets, balances and common financial-assistant FAQs.

## What is a pending transaction?
A pending transaction has been initiated but has not completed successfully yet. Its amount should not be treated as a final completed debit until the transaction status becomes successful.

## What is a spending category?
A spending category groups transactions such as Food & Dining, Shopping, Travel, Bills & Utilities, Entertainment, Healthcare and Education.

## How is monthly spending calculated?
Monthly spending is calculated by summing successful debit transactions within the requested month.

## What data does this demo use?
This portfolio prototype uses synthetic transaction data. It does not use real customer financial information.
